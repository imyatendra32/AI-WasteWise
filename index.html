🎯 Sustainable Development Goal

AI WasteWise primarily supports SDG 12 – Responsible Consumption and Production by encouraging better waste segregation, recycling and responsible disposal.

🛡️ Responsible AI

AI-generated recommendations are intended as guidance. Waste-management rules can vary by location. Users should verify special disposal requirements with their local waste-management authority.

The project aims to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information and provide transparent recommendations.
